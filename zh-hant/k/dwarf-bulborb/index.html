小查比Dwarf Bulborb

小查比是系列最常見的小型敵人,長得像幼年查比,其實是麵包蟲家族的擬態種;丟一隻皮克敏就能解決。

重點
  • 二代名為小紅查比(Dwarf Red Bulborb)
  • 外觀模仿查比以躲避天敵,實為麵包蟲(breadbug)家族
  • 很弱,丟一隻皮克敏即可擊倒
原文(英文)

The Dwarf Bulborb (コチャッピー, lit.: "Small Chappy"), named the Dwarf Red Bulborb (アカコチャッピー, lit.: "Red Small Chappy") in Pikmin 2, is a small and common enemy in the Pikmin series. They are small bipedal creatures equipped with a pair of eyes on stalks, two fangs on either side of their mouth, two toes on each leg; each having a single claw, and a curved snout. Their bodies are beige-colored and their backs are red, which are covered with white spots. Dwarf Bulborbs strongly resemble juvenile Bulborbs. However, they are actually a different species in the breadbug family, and mimic the appearance of Bulborbs to evade predation. They are rather weak and can be easily defeated by throwing a single Pikmin directly on top of them. Exclusively in the first game, pestering one for long enough will prompt it to scream for help, waking up the nearest Spotty Bulborb (or Spotty Bulbear in Challenge Mode).
