皮克敏是什麼(物種總覽)Pikmin family

皮克敏是 PNF-404 上半植物半動物的生物:洋蔥產出種子、落地成芽,隊長拔起後就會跟著你,可以投擲、解散、群攻、衝鋒,吹哨能召回。

重點
  • 半植物半動物,棲息於 PNF-404(Hey! Pikmin 則在另一顆星球)
  • 由洋蔥產出種子 → 落地成芽 → 隊長拔起才會加入隊伍
  • 指令:投擲、解散、群攻、衝鋒;離隊時吹哨召回
原文(英文)

Pikmin (ピクミン) are plant-animal hybrid creatures that are found on PNF-404 and the planet in Hey! Pikmin. As the titular creatures of the Pikmin series, they are the main feature of gameplay in all games in the series. In the main games, Pikmin are created by Onions, which produce them as seeds, which in turn fall to the ground and become sprouts. A leader can then pluck one of these sprouts. Once plucked, Pikmin follow a leader around in their squad, and the leader can throw them at objects, as well as dismiss, swarm, and charge them. When out of a leader's squad, they can be whistled back into it. Through these basic actions, Pikmin can be ordered to carry items or fight enemies, among other abilities. Pikmin come in several types, each with certain unique abilities (such as resistance to a specific hazard). Most are 1.2cm tall head to toe (around 3cm if the stem and top are included), and have a generally humanoid shape. On top of a Pikmin's head is either a leaf, bud, or flower, representing its maturity. On its own, a Pikmin is not particularly strong, but in large groups (usually up to 100), they are capable of accomplishing many things. Pikmin can be lost to enemies and hazards, and losing all Pikmin, either of a specific type or in total, is known as a Pikmin extinction. However, Pikmin can be grown easily by carrying defeated enemies or pellets to the Onion. In addition, Candypop Buds can convert Pikmin from one type to another.
