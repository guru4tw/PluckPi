第一次接觸皮克敏要先知道的事:這是什麼遊戲、基本單位是什麼、一天怎麼過。 共 6 條,預設難度「入門」。

入門

一天的循環與日落Day

主系列以「一天」為單位推進,夜晚有危險生物,探索只在白天;一代限 30 天、三代看果汁存量最多 99 天,二、四代無天數限制。

重點
  • 夜間危險生物出沒,探索只在白天進行
  • 一代:歐利瑪(Olimar)30 天內要修好維生系統,否則永困星球
  • 三代:剩餘天數取決於果汁瓶數,上限 99 天
  • 二代與四代沒有天數上限(四代「歐利瑪漂流記」模式限 15 天)
原文(英文)

The main Pikmin games take place over the course of several in-game days (日, lit.: "Day"). Dangerous creatures roam at nighttime, so exploration on PNF-404 generally only happens during the daytime. The number of days spent on the planet is also important in some games: in Pikmin, Captain Olimar has 30 days before his life-support systems fail and he's stranded on the planet permanently, and in Pikmin 3, the number of days left is determined by how many bottles of fruit juice are left, for a maximum of 99 days. Pikmin 2 and Pikmin 4 have no day limit (though the alternative game mode "Olimar's Shipwreck Tale" in Pikmin 4 has a 15 day time limit), and in New Play Control! Pikmin, the Nintendo Switch port of Pikmin, and Pikmin 3, the player is given the ability to go back in time and retry from a certain day of their adventure, through an option in the saved game selection menu in the case of New Play Control! Pikmin and the Nintendo Switch port of Pikmin, or a dedicated day selection menu in the case of Pikmin 3. A common challenge run in the Pikmin games is to complete the game taking the least amount of days possible. The Sun Meter at the top of the HUD indicates the amount of daylight left, or in Pikmin 4's case, the clock, so effectively, the time the player has left on the current day. A number on the top right corner indicates the current day. In the Story Mode of Pikmin games, a day lasts between 13 and 18 real-life minutes, depending on the game, version, and difficulty mode, as explained here. Alternative game modes have days with varying lengths. As the day goes by, the lighting in the area changes much as it does on Earth, but enemy behavior and area layout remains constant throughout (save for the Serene Shores in Pikmin 4 where the tide changes at noon). Any important collectible object, such as a treasure, that is moved from its original spot, will remain in the new spot on any following day. In Pikmin, Pikmin 2, and Pikmin 3, when an enemy is killed, it usually respawns in its original place after some number of days, and if an enemy's corpse isn't brought back to an Onion before the end of the day, the corpse will disappear. In Pikmin 4, enemy corpses will remain in the location they were left at every day until brought back to an Onion, and enemies in overground areas do not respawn. Some enemies only appear on certain days. Some of the most notable enemies with this characteristic are the Smoky Progg, which disappears after day 15, the Ujadani, which only appear every 30 days, and the Goolix and Mamuta on The Impact Site, that appear on alternating days. For a list of all enemies that appear on exclusive days, please read the list of enemies in the corresponding area article.

洋蔥(皮克敏的母艦)Onion

洋蔥(Onion)是皮克敏的母艦,一種顏色一顆;寄生與發光皮克敏沒有洋蔥。三代起已發現的洋蔥會合體成一顆。

重點
  • 八種主要洋蔥:紅、黃、藍、紫、白、岩石、飛行、冰
  • 到四代為止,寄生皮克敏(Bulbmin)與發光皮克敏沒有已知的洋蔥
  • 三代中發現的洋蔥會在當晚合併成一顆通用洋蔥,收放多色皮克敏更方便
原文(英文)

Onions (オニヨン, lit.: "Oniyon") are large, round life forms that act as mother ships for the Pikmin. There are eight main types of Onion nests, each with unique coloration corresponding to either Red, Yellow, Blue, Purple, White, Rock, Winged, and Ice. As of Pikmin 4, Bulbmin and Glow Pikmin are the only Pikmin types to have no known Onions. In Pikmin 3, all discovered Onions merge together the night after they are discovered, and become a single, universal Onion, greatly simplifying the act of withdrawing and retrieving multiple Pikmin types at once. It also appears that the Onions in this game are much more free in regards to which Pikmin species they will hold than their Pikmin and Pikmin 2 counterparts. In Pikmin 3, if a new Onion color is discovered, and Pikmin from that new type are left on the landing site at the end of the day, the master Onion there will accommodate them until the Onions fuse at the end of the day. In Pikmin 4, it's revealed they are part of the candypop family. Onions merge in this game much like in Pikmin 3, but discovered Onions remain dormant upon being found and must be manually carried back to the master Onion in order for them to merge. Since some Pikmin types have duplicate Onions in Pikmin 4, collecting a type that has already been obtained will result in its absorption by the master Onion, converting it into 20 Pikmin of the Onion's color. Flarlic can also be brought to the Onion to increase the Pikmin limit by 10. During the daytime, Pikmin hunt and bring food, such as pellets or defeated enemies, to their Onion, which in turn produces new Pikmin seeds. If there are fewer Pikmin on the field than the allowed Pikmin limit, the seeds are ejected from its flower's head and take root in the ground as sprouts; if not, they are added to that type's Pikmin reserves as leaf Pikmin. Unlike in the area, however, the number of Pikmin that can be stored inside an Onion is unlimited, as a general rule. When night falls and all the Pikmin have returned, the Onion folds or sucks in its legs and lifts off from the ground, joining the ship in low orbit.

皮克敏是什麼(物種總覽)Pikmin family

皮克敏是 PNF-404 上半植物半動物的生物:洋蔥產出種子、落地成芽,隊長拔起後就會跟著你,可以投擲、解散、群攻、衝鋒,吹哨能召回。

重點
  • 半植物半動物,棲息於 PNF-404(Hey! Pikmin 則在另一顆星球)
  • 由洋蔥產出種子 → 落地成芽 → 隊長拔起才會加入隊伍
  • 指令:投擲、解散、群攻、衝鋒;離隊時吹哨召回
原文(英文)

Pikmin (ピクミン) are plant-animal hybrid creatures that are found on PNF-404 and the planet in Hey! Pikmin. As the titular creatures of the Pikmin series, they are the main feature of gameplay in all games in the series. In the main games, Pikmin are created by Onions, which produce them as seeds, which in turn fall to the ground and become sprouts. A leader can then pluck one of these sprouts. Once plucked, Pikmin follow a leader around in their squad, and the leader can throw them at objects, as well as dismiss, swarm, and charge them. When out of a leader's squad, they can be whistled back into it. Through these basic actions, Pikmin can be ordered to carry items or fight enemies, among other abilities. Pikmin come in several types, each with certain unique abilities (such as resistance to a specific hazard). Most are 1.2cm tall head to toe (around 3cm if the stem and top are included), and have a generally humanoid shape. On top of a Pikmin's head is either a leaf, bud, or flower, representing its maturity. On its own, a Pikmin is not particularly strong, but in large groups (usually up to 100), they are capable of accomplishing many things. Pikmin can be lost to enemies and hazards, and losing all Pikmin, either of a specific type or in total, is known as a Pikmin extinction. However, Pikmin can be grown easily by carrying defeated enemies or pellets to the Onion. In addition, Candypop Buds can convert Pikmin from one type to another.

隊長(玩家操作的角色)Leader

隊長(Leader)是玩家操作的角色,靠投擲與哨聲指揮皮克敏;全系列 11 位,多數是外星探險者。

重點
  • 指揮方式:投擲、吹哨等動作
  • 全系列共 11 位隊長,其中歐慶(Oatchi)與 Moss 是太空狗
  • 部分作品可切換多位隊長,同時管理多支隊伍
  • 一代只有歐利瑪(Olimar):Hocotate 運輸的員工,迫降在 PNF-404
原文(英文)

Leaders (リーダー, lit.: "Leader"), unofficially also called captains, are the characters the player controls in the Pikmin games. Leaders have the ability to lead a squad of Pikmin, and can command the squad through throwing, whistling, and other actions. Each of the 11 leaders in the series is an alien explorer with some role in the mission, with the exception of Oatchi and Moss who are space dogs. Some games have multiple leaders which can be switched between, allowing multiple squads to be managed at a time. In Pikmin, there is only one leader, Olimar, an employee of Hocotate Freight who crash-landed on PNF-404. In Pikmin 2, Louie is added as a second playable character; it is possible to switch between the two playable leaders by pressing / / . Louie is replaced by the President after the debt is cleared. In Pikmin 3 there are three new leaders: the engineer Alph, the botanist Brittany, and the crew's captain, Charlie. / / is used to switch in the original Pikmin 3 and the action menu left and right is used to switch in Pikmin 3 Deluxe. Pikmin 4 has several playable leaders across its game modes, though the main story features the rookie Rescue Officer and Oatchi as the leaders. Hey! Pikmin again features only Olimar as a playable character. Puffstools, Antenna Beetles, mature Bulbmin, the Scornet Maestro, and Moss act as naturally occurring enemy leaders, in each of the four main Pikmin games, stealing the player's Pikmin or scouting with their own type of underlings.

顆粒(增加皮克敏的基本資源)Pellet

顆粒(Pellet)是結晶化的花蜜,搬回洋蔥就能長出皮克敏,是開局最主要的種子來源。

重點
  • 顆粒上的數字代表重量,有 1、5、8、10、20 五種
  • 顏色與洋蔥相同時,產出同數量的皮克敏(1 號例外,產 2 隻)
  • 顏色不同時只產一半
原文(英文)

A pellet (ペレット, lit.: "Pellet") is a crystallized chunk of nectar that contains vitamins and other nutrition. Pellets are used to produce Pikmin, by bringing them to the Onion, and are the main source of seeds at the start of the games, as large creatures require many Pikmin to kill safely. Pellets come in a variety of sizes, and each one produces a different amount of Pikmin. An Arabic numeral is seen atop each one, reflecting its weight. Pellets with the numbers 1, 5, 8, 10, and 20 exist. If the color of the pellet matches the color of the Onion it is brought to, that same number of Pikmin is produced (the exception being the number 1, which creates two Pikmin). If not, only half that number, rounded up, will appear. According to the Japanese version of Pikmin 2, this is due to a better absorption of the nutrients in the pellet. They are found on the top of Pellet Posies and sometimes on the ground. According to the Japanese Pikmin Garden website, pellets are a type of fruit. Certain creatures of PNF-404 also leave pellets as spoils at times. They have presumably eaten pellets, which could indicate the possibility that the same creatures are herbivores, if not omnivores.
