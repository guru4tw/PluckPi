2皮克敏圖鑑

各色皮克敏的特性、免疫與用途。選對顏色就是成功的一半。 共 10 條,預設難度「入門」。

入門

紅色皮克敏Red Pikmin

紅色皮克敏耐火、攻擊力強,是每一代最先遇到的皮克敏,公認的「基本款」。

重點
  • 免疫火焰
  • 攻擊力強
  • 由紅色洋蔥培育,花苞與花朵為白色
  • 全系列所有模式都登場,僅四代夜間探索(發光皮克敏專屬)除外
原文(英文)

Red Pikmin (赤ピクミン) are a red species of Pikmin with the special abilities of fire resistance and strong attack power. They are the first Pikmin type seen or discovered in all games by the main playable leaders, and are usually considered the "main" type of Pikmin due to their relatively simple traits and early discovery. They are cultivated via the Red Onion (or the master Onion), and like Yellow Pikmin, Blue Pikmin and Bulbmin, their buds and flowers are white. These Pikmin are present in all games of the series, and make an appearance in all game modes, excluding Pikmin 4's night expeditions, which are Glow Pikmin-exclusive.

藍色皮克敏Blue Pikmin

藍色皮克敏能在水中活動,一到三代都是最後發現的種類;粉紅色的嘴是牠們獨有的特徵。

重點
  • 免疫水,可在水下與陸上呼吸
  • 一、二、三代故事模式最後發現的種類;四代則是第四種
  • 獨有的粉紅色嘴巴用來吸水、經鰓過濾氧氣
原文(英文)

Blue Pikmin (青ピクミン) are a blue species of Pikmin that reside on PNF-404. They have the special ability of water resistance. This type of Pikmin is present in all game modes of all Pikmin games, and is the last type to be discovered in the Story mode of Pikmin, Pikmin 2, and Pikmin 3, as well as the fourth type discovered in Pikmin 4. Besides their coloration, these Pikmin are distinguishable from other Pikmin species by their pink mouths, a trait seen in no other Pikmin species. These mouths are used to take in water, which filters oxygen through gills, giving them the ability to breathe both underwater and on dry land. Producing more Blue Pikmin can be done by carrying pellets or enemy carcasses to the Blue Onion (or the Master Onion), or by throwing other species into a Lapis Lazuli Candypop Bud or a Candypop Bud. As with Red Pikmin, Yellow Pikmin and Bulbmin, their buds and flowers are white. The exact location of their gills is not consistently explained in the series, having been said to be on the cheeks, near the cheeks, their mouths, and not even visible. Pikmin 4 consistently says that the mouths are the gills.

黃色皮克敏Yellow Pikmin

黃色皮克敏丟得比較高,二代起免疫電擊;一代還能搬炸彈岩。

重點
  • 投擲高度較高,可拿到高處的太空船零件
  • 一代可搬運炸彈岩(bomb-rock)
  • 一代之後的每一作都免疫電
  • 像大耳朵的葉片(lamellae)是辨識特徵
原文(英文)

Yellow Pikmin (黄ピクミン) are a yellow species of Pikmin, with the special abilities of increased throw height, bomb-rock carrying (in Pikmin), and electricity resistance (in every game after Pikmin). They are the second type discovered by Captain Olimar in Pikmin, the fourth type identified in Pikmin 2, and the first type found by Captain Charlie in Pikmin 3. In Pikmin 4, Pikmin can be discovered in any order (with the exception of Red Pikmin). These Pikmin have lamellae, which resemble large ears; these are the means by which they are primarily identifiable aside from their yellow-coloring. Yellow Pikmin can be thrown higher – an ability that is needed to retrieve certain elevated ship parts, treasures and fruits – and are immune to electricity. Additionally, Yellow Pikmin appear to like being in elevated places, such as trees – this can be concluded from the places they are first found in Pikmin 2 and Pikmin 3. Their main method of production is through the Yellow Onion (or the Master Onion), and like Red Pikmin, Blue Pikmin, and Bulbmin, their buds and flowers are white. Also like Red Pikmin and Blue Pikmin, they appear in all games of the series, and the only game mode they are missing from in comparison to the other two, is 2-Player Battle.

冰凍皮克敏Ice Pikmin

冰凍皮克敏(四代、Bloom)免疫冰凍,能把敵人和水面、泥地凍住;但攻擊力較低、遇火燒得更快。

重點

原文(英文)

Ice Pikmin (氷ピクミン) are a type of Pikmin that appear in Pikmin 4 and Pikmin Bloom. They have light blue and blocky bodies, seemingly made of chunks of ice. Their defining characteristics are their immunity to all types of freezing, and their ability to freeze enemies and bodies of water and mud. They have lower attack power in battle, and burn to death faster than other Pikmin types when lit aflame by sources of fire. They leave small snow and water vapor particles around them as they move, and their buds and flowers are yellow.

岩石皮克敏Rock Pikmin

岩石皮克敏(三代起)像顆小石頭,免疫壓碎與刺穿,攻擊方式也與其他皮克敏不同。

重點

  • 登場:三代、四代、Hey! Pikmin、Pikmin Bloom
原文(英文)

Rock Pikmin (岩ピクミン) are a species of Pikmin appearing in Pikmin 3, Pikmin 4, Hey! Pikmin and Pikmin Bloom, with the special abilities of crush (on soft surfaces) and stab immunity and a different method of attacking. They resemble small polished rocks with Pikmin eyes and limbs, and have lavender-purple buds and flowers like the Winged Pikmin. The main part of their bodies seem to be a dark gray texture of graphite that reflects light, whereas their stems, arms, and legs appear to be smooth and gray. When Rock Pikmin are dismissed, their sprouts glow gray, oddily from Pikmin 4, they glow as a grayish cyan. Their ghosts are also shaped like small gray rocks with eyes. Just like all other types of Pikmin in Pikmin 3, Rock Pikmin have an Onion, which later in the story merges with the Master Onion, and are reproduced through collecting nutrients from pellets and corpses. In Hey! Pikmin, like all other types, they are found in certain levels and go to the Pikmin Park after a level is done.

發光皮克敏Glow Pikmin

發光皮克敏是四代夜間探索專用:會漂浮、瞬移到玩家身邊、合體成會爆炸的 Glowmob,抗多數危害,但見不得太陽。

  • 抗多數危害,但白天戶外無法使用

原文(英文)

Glow Pikmin (ヒカリピクミン, lit.: "Light Pikmin") are a type of Pikmin found in Pikmin 4. They are unlocked and mainly used during night expeditions, though they can also be summoned in caves via Glow Seeds and in Dandori Battles via the Quick Glow Pik item. Because of their ghostly traits, they are able to float slightly above ground, teleport to the player's side, meld themselves into an explosive blob called a Glowmob, and resist most manners of hazard. On the other hand, they cannot be out in the sun, and so cannot be used when outdoors during the day. As of Pikmin 4, Glow Pikmin are the only Pikmin types to not have an Onion or an associated Candypop Bud. Rather, they inhabit in Lumiknolls.

白色皮克敏White Pikmin

白色皮克敏跑得快、免疫毒,還能挖出完全埋在地下的東西;二代起登場,體型最小、紅眼。

重點

  • 免疫毒
  • 能找出完全埋在地下的物品
  • 二代只能透過特定的 Candypop Bud 取得
原文(英文)

White Pikmin (白ピクミン) are a type of Pikmin found in Pikmin 2, Pikmin 4, Pikmin Bloom, and non-story game modes in Pikmin 3. They are the third type of Pikmin found in Pikmin 2, the first of which are found in the White Flower Garden. Their most noteworthy characteristics are their faster speed compared to other Pikmin, ability to find buried objects that are completely hidden underground, and their immunity to poison. Their defining physical characteristics are their red eyes, white color, and smaller size. White Pikmin share a number of similarities with Purple Pikmin. Their flowers (as well as buds) are magenta rather than white. In Pikmin 2, they can only be obtained via certain Candypop Buds, in this case Ivory Candypop Buds, and they are stored in the Hocotate ship. In Pikmin 4, they are stored in the master Onion, and a White Onion can be obtained to produce White Pikmin by completing the 5th story of the Trial of the Sage Leaf.

紫色皮克敏的重量與搬運力是一般皮克敏的 10 倍,但速度慢;二代有獨特的重擊攻擊,部分寶物非牠不可。

原文(英文)

Purple Pikmin (紫ピクミン) are a type of Pikmin found in Pikmin 2, Pikmin 4, Pikmin Bloom, and non-story game modes in Pikmin 3. They are the second type of Pikmin found in Pikmin 2, the first of which are found in the Emergence Cave. Their most noteworthy characteristics are their massive weight and carrying strength – 10 times those of a regular Pikmin. They are also slower than other Pikmin types, and have a unique way of attacking enemies in Pikmin 2 through a pound. They are specifically required to obtain a number of treasures, two of which are needed to gain access to new areas, making them necessary to complete the game. Their defining physical characteristics are their purple color, as the name would imply, as well as their bulbous physique when compared to other Pikmin, in addition to the straight trichomes atop their heads. Similarly to White Pikmin, Purple Pikmin feature magenta buds and flowers, rather than white. In Pikmin 2, Purple Pikmin do not have an Onion and can only be harvested through Violet Candypop Buds, found in various caves. At the end of each day, they are stored in the Hocotate ship, much like White Pikmin in the same game. They do not have an Onion in Pikmin 3 either, appearing in non-story game modes only. In Pikmin 4, they are also only harvestable with Purple Candypop Buds, but when the Purple Onion is obtained by completing the final story of the Trial of the Sage Leaf, they can be grown like the other Pikmin types.

原文(英文)

Winged Pikmin (羽ピクミン) are a Pikmin type that appear in Pikmin 3, Pikmin 4, Hey! Pikmin, and Pikmin Bloom, with the special ability of flight. They are pink with large blue eyes, their bodies have two dark pink stripes, resembling real-world bees, and they have lavender-purple buds and flowers, like Rock Pikmin. Their name comes from the transparent wings on their backs, which allow them to be airborne most of the time. This is particularly useful in combat and while carrying. Like other main Pikmin types, they are produced in an Onion and appear in all of the game's modes.

寄生皮克敏Bulbmin

寄生皮克敏(二代限定)是寄生在查比身上的皮克敏,只在洞窟出現、無法帶出,四種危害全免疫。

重點

  • 免疫遊戲定義的四種危害
  • 剛出現時是敵人:成體像紅查比,後面跟著最多 10 隻幼體
  • 是洞窟中少數能增加皮克敏的方式
原文(英文)

Bulbmin (adults: ハチャッピー, lit.: "Leaf Chappy", juvenile: コッパチャッピー, lit.: "Small Leaf Chappy") are a parasitic species of Pikmin in Pikmin 2 that infect Bulborbs, hence the name. They are only found in caves and there is no way to store them, meaning Bulbmin can only be used in the cave they are found in. They are resistant to all four hazards defined by the game, and are one of the only ways to gain Pikmin in a cave, making them a valuable asset. Uniquely for a Pikmin type, they start out as enemies when they appear in a cave. An adult Bulbmin, closely resembling a Red Bulborb, is followed by up to 10 juvenile Bulbmin, which take on a more unique appearance (having completely black eyes rather than the blue or purple eyes of the dwarf and true Bulborbs, uniquely proportioned and colored legs, and relatively fewer and larger white spots which are positioned differently than those of other Bulborbs) with a stem and leaf (or bud or flower) atop their back. They can be found wandering the floors of certain caves and behave much like the Spotty Bulbear, patrolling an established path. The adult Bulbmin is quite aggressive, but can be defeated quickly. When the adult Bulbmin is defeated, the juvenile Bulbmin will panic until whistled, at which point they will join the player's squad. At this point, they can be used like normal Pikmin. When the player uses a geyser to exit the cave, all Bulbmin in their squad are left behind. It is implied that other creatures can be infected by the parasitic species of Pikmin.
